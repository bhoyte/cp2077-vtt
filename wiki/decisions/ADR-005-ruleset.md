# ADR-005 — Ruleset

**Context.** The spec runs Cyberpunk RED rules directly. R. Talsorian's homebrew policy forbids reproducing rules text
and charging for derived content (R-08). The prior project's homebrew ([ruleset bible](../../raw/night-city-live/night-city-live-ruleset-design.md))
fixed the IP problem but abstracted initiative away and assumed a single protagonist. This show needs initiative order:
turn order *is* the squad structure.

**Decision.** Keep RED's *shape* (d10 + stat + specialty vs DV, exploding 10s, armor that chips, a death save each turn
at 0 HP), with original names, numbers, and tables. Concrete draft: [design/ruleset](../design/ruleset.md). All numbers
are v0, set by the balance simulator before launch, and frozen for a season.

**Seat 4 rename.** The streamer's persona is "the Fixer who hands out gigs" (philosophy doc), and seat 4 is also "Fixer".
Two Fixers confuse chat. Seat 4 becomes **Face** (talker, dealmaker, favors). The streamer stays the Fixer/handler.

**Consequences.** No R. Talsorian text, tables, or role-ability names appear anywhere. The overlay shows our own terms.
