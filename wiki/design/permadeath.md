# Permadeath

## Flatline sequence (≈ 25 s)
1. Failed death save: the dice overlay holds on the roll, with a "VERIFY: roll #1847" chip (ADR-006).
2. Beat `flatline`: slow blend to the body; the actor goes to its dead state (ragdoll or death pose, per S-03); the scene's color grade desaturates via the overlay.
3. OBS: full-screen **FLATLINED** banner, heartbeat SFX stops.
4. Memorial: this character's pre-rendered vignette plays under a live HTML epitaph with name, handle, gigs survived, kills, last roll, **who voted for the fatal action**, and the Tech squad's last move.
5. Name added to the **Afterlife Wall** (persistent overlay page + lobby screen).

## Memorials are rendered at birth
The spec pre-renders generic memorial clips. A generic clip can't show *this* character, so:
- At character creation, the stage loads a "portrait booth" scene, the orchestrator grabs an OBS screenshot of the pawn,
  and queues an image-to-video job (Hailuo; the workflow already exists in `D:\cppc\docs`) for a 6–10 s noir vignette.
- Stored at `media/memorials/<character_id>.mp4`. Until it's ready, fall back to a slow push on the portrait still.
- Everything that changes with the run (stats, kills, voters) is live HTML on top, so the video never needs regenerating.

## Replacement
- The seat is locked; the whole crew votes three 20 s lifepath polls (origin · motive · enemy). The enemy result seeds future encounters (spec idea, kept).
- Pawn: pick from 3 curated catalog looks per seat (no live appearance validation, F-005).
- Rookie stats = seat template + lifepath modifiers, generated by the dice service on screen.
- The rookie joins at the next encounter boundary, never mid-fight.
